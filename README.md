# VLCC Special Section — Speculative Discussions on the Fate of Time-Matter

This repository presents speculative research developed within the **VLCC model** (*Variable Lagrangian Cosmological Model of the Time Field*), created by **Vronsky**.  
In this framework, time is treated as a **dynamic matter-field**, carrying its own energy and measurable structure, governed by two fundamental speculative laws:  
- **LPHD** — *Law of Dynamic Horizon Pulsation*  
- **LCFT** — *Law of Conservation of Temporal Flux*

### 🧭 Contents
- `Trois_Hypotheses_sur_le_Destin_du_Temps_VLCC.docx`: main speculative discussion document  
- Complementary texts: analyses, appendices, and forthcoming VLCC developments  
- License and references

### 🧪 Abstract
Three hypotheses are explored regarding the nature and fate of time:  
1. An **infinite time**, inexhaustible and stable.  
2. A **transitional time**, evolving naturally according to *“nothing is lost, everything transforms.”*  
3. An **exhaustible time**, whose pulsation may cease, detectable via LPHD and LCFT laws.
